Mobile-first redesign
=====================
This version was rebuilt around the phone experience first.

Key changes:
- Full-screen mobile hero
- Thumb-friendly menu and directions actions
- Swipeable story cards
- Compact popular-item list
- Large touch targets
- Mobile app-style bottom navigation
- Header hides while scrolling down
- Stronger emotional storytelling
- Faster visual hierarchy
- Less decorative clutter
- Better safe-area handling
- Desktop layout expands from the mobile system rather than replacing it
