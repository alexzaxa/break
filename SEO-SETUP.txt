IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING
===========================
1. Replace the domain "https://break-coffee-food.example" everywhere if your real domain is different.
2. Replace the placeholder phone number in the JSON-LD schema if you add one.
3. Verify the site in Google Search Console.
4. Submit: https://break-coffee-food.example/sitemap.xml
5. Claim/verify the Google Business Profile and use the exact same name/address.
6. Add the live website URL to the Google Business Profile.

No website can guarantee first place in Google. Ranking depends on competition,
Google Business Profile quality, reviews, proximity, authority, and indexing.
